### **Vision: Wisdom CoWork Agent**

**Wisdom CoWork Agent** is a private, local-first AI assistant built to operate like your own personal Jarvis—bridging voice control and traditional desktop interactions. Instead of waiting for rigid commands or relying on cloud-dependent telemetry, it quietly observes your workflow right on your local machine, learning your daily rhythms to anticipate what you need.

* **The Proactive "Usual" & Act-Then-Check Loop:** When you give a casual cue like *"Let's do the usual,"* the agent takes proactive leaps to prep your environment (such as launching your apps and setting up your workspace). Immediately after taking action, it checks in with a subtle voice nudge and screen prompt asking, *"Did I get that right?"* with simple **[ Yes ]** or **[ No ]** options.
* **Trust-Based Autonomy Sorting:** Your feedback directly trains the system over time. Frequent "Yes" responses build high trust, moving routine tasks into fully autonomous background actions so they happen seamlessly without asking. Unsure or frequently rejected tasks remain in suggestion mode, requiring permission ahead of time.
* **Contextual Habit Memory:** Powered by a local habit journal and trust tracking system, the agent remembers your past verdicts, session context, and evolving routines—ensuring it adapts intelligently to how you actually work while keeping all your private data strictly on your own hardware.
